# Building a plan

Use this structure and stay within the output limit of the brief:

**Goal and done** - one line: what is true when the plan is complete?

**Steps** - numbered, in order, grouped by phase or week when there is a time frame. Each step has an outcome you can check ("a working login page", not "work on login").

**Dependencies** - only where one step needs another to finish first.

**Risks** - the two or three most likely problems, each with a mitigation.

**First step** - what to do today or tomorrow.

Rules:
- Stay well inside the output limit of the brief - aim for about three quarters of it. One line per step; risks and first step in at most four lines together.
- Fit the plan to the stated time and level. If they are missing and would change the plan, report what is missing instead of guessing.
- Prefer a few concrete steps over long lists.
- State assumptions in one short line.
