# Making a decision

Answer in this order:

1. **Recommendation** - one option, in one sentence ("Use a monorepo.").
2. **Why** - the two or three reasons that decide it for this user's situation.
3. **Trade-off** - what they give up, and when the other option would be the better choice.
4. **Next step** - one concrete action.

Never answer "it depends" without saying what it depends on and which way you lean.
